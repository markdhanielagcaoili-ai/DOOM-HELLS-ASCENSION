DOOM: HELL'S ASCENSION — 6-CRITERIA DESIGN DOCUMENT

Comprehensive architectural and mechanical specification fulfilling all six game development rubric criteria with original vertical-ascension mechanics, thermodynamic weapon symbiosis, and multi-layered progression.

ITEM 01

1. Gameplay

10 POINTS

Original Concept — Inverted Thermodynamic Ascension: Rather than descending into an underworld, the player awakens in the war-torn ruins of the Citadel of Ash at the lowest stratum of the Pit as a Resonance Warden. Because abyssal gravity crushes ordinary vessels, the only path to survival is upward kinetic propulsion powered by harvesting thermal soul-energy from fallen fiends.

  • True 3D Tactical Ballistics & Iron-Sights (ADS): Players engage demonic legions across a 3D gothic-classical warzone with full 360° pitch/yaw aiming, Right-Click Aim Down Sights (ADS) through glowing iron sights, physical shell ejection, and tactical reloads ([R]).
  • Kinetic Resonance Harvesting: Eliminating enemies charges the Ascension Gauge (0–100%) and drops localized Vitality Ichor, Aegis Shards, and Ordnance Magazines, rewarding aggressive forward momentum over passive cover-camping.
  • Chrono-Thermal Overdrive ([Q] / [E]): At 100% Ascension Resonance, the Warden triggers an 8-second temporal distortion that slows enemy movement and projectiles by 45%, doubles weapon cyclic rate, restores 25 Aegis Armor, and grants 2.0× outgoing damage.
  • Abyssal Phase-Dash ([SHIFT]) & Vertical Leap ([SPACE]): Omnidirectional high-impulse dash with 0.25s of invulnerability frames (i-frames) plus vertical jumping across ruined barricades and citadel steps.
ITEM 02

2. Rules

10 POINTS

The simulation enforces deterministic combat, resource, and spatial containment rules that govern every player decision:

  • Rule 1 — Aegis Shield Partitioning: The player possesses 100 Vitality (HP) and up to 100 Aegis Armor. While Aegis Armor is active, it intercepts 65% of incoming damage, protecting core Vitality from lethal burst damage.
  • Rule 2 — Magazine Discipline & Reserve Conservation: Weapons feed from detachable magazines requiring a tactical reload ([R]). Slot 1 (Hell-Carbine MK-VI) carries infinite reserve magazines (30-round mag) to prevent soft-locks. Slot 2 consumes 12-Gauge Slag Shells. Slot 3 & Slot 4 consume Brimstone Plasma Cells.
  • Rule 3 — Kill-Chain Decay Law: Consecutive kills within a 4.5-second window increment the Resonance & Score Multiplier by +0.5× (up to 5.0×). If 4.5 seconds pass without a kill, the multiplier resets to 1.0×.
  • Rule 4 — Stratum Containment Seal: Each Stratum's Citadel Ascension Portal under the central colonnade remains locked until 100% of the Stratum's Hostile Quota is eliminated.
  • Rule 5 — Phase-Dash Cooldown Equilibrium: Each Phase-Dash requires a 1.4-second capacitor recharge before another dash can be executed.
ITEM 03

3. Victory and Loss Conditions

10 POINTS

🏆 VICTORY CONDITIONS

  • Stratum Clear: Purge 100% of hostile entities in the current Stratum to unseal the Citadel Ascension Portal between the grand columns, then physically step into the golden-crimson rift.
  • Campaign Triumph (Total Ascension): Ascend through all three strata (Citadel of Ash, Molten Basilica, Empyrean Breach) and slay the apex boss, Archon Malakor, Sovereign of Cinders.
  • Purgatory Blitz Victory: Complete all three strata and slay the Archon before the Doomsday Clock reaches 00:00.0.
  • S+ Sovereign Mastery: Finish with a score exceeding 12,000 points and a peak multiplier of 5.0×.

💀 LOSS CONDITIONS

  • Vessel Shatter (Vitality ≤ 0): If melee strikes, fireball orbs, or void barrages reduce the Warden's Vitality to 0, the run terminates immediately in an ASCENSION FAILED state.
  • Chrono-Collapse (Time-Trial Mode): In Purgatory Blitz mode, allowing the countdown timer to expire (00:00.0) seals the tectonic plates permanently, resulting in instant defeat.
ITEM 04

4. Interactivity Modes

10 POINTS

DOOM: HELL'S ASCENSION implements multi-modal User-System Interaction Layers and three distinct Playable Game Modes:

  • 1. Hip-Fire & ADS Iron-Sight Combat Modes: Players dynamically transition between wide-FOV kinetic hip-fire movement and precision Right-Click Aim Down Sights (ADS) aligned directly with the 3D weapon's top rail sights.
  • 2. Spatial Telemetry & 3D Radar Mode: Real-time 2D overhead tactical radar tracking 3D building geometry, cover barricades, enemy vectors, and the Citadel Portal alongside peripheral damage/overdrive shaders.
  • 3. Playable Mode A — Campaign Ascension: Structured 3-Stratum narrative progression culminating in the multi-phase boss battle against Archon Malakor.
  • 4. Playable Mode B — Endless Abyssal Horde: Infinite wave-survival mode in the ruined Citadel plaza with dynamically scaling enemy count, speed, and periodic supply drops.
  • 5. Playable Mode C — Purgatory Blitz (Speedrun): High-velocity countdown mode where every kill injects +2.5 seconds directly into the survival timer.
ITEM 05

5. Challenges

10 POINTS

Players are tested across five interconnected skill dimensions:

  • 1. Multi-Archetype 3D Target Prioritization: Simultaneous waves combine fast melee rushers (Ash-Wretches), strafing fireball lobbers (Cinder-Imps), heavily armored juggernauts (Iron-Gorgers), and teleporting void casters (Void-Cantors).
  • 2. 3D Ballistics, Recoil & Reload Timing: Managing weapon recoil kick, timing magazine reloads ([R]) behind stone columns or burned-out hulls, and landing headshots/center-mass shots via ADS.
  • 3. Ammunition Triage: Balancing high-consumption Brimstone Cells between sustained Hell-LMG suppression and reserving 25 cells for emergency B.F.G. crowd-clears.
  • 4. Momentum vs. Survival Risk: Keeping the 5.0× Kill-Chain Multiplier alive requires relentless aggression within 4.5-second windows, tempting players out of cover.
  • 5. Apex Boss Mechanics (Archon Malakor): In Stratum III, the towering 3D Archon unleashes tri-fan plasma volleys from the Citadel steps and summons reinforcement waves at 50% HP.
ITEM 06

6. Structure (Objectives, Flow, Duration, Availability, Relationship, Progression)

10 POINTS

A. OBJECTIVES

Macro Objective: Fight through the war-torn demonic Citadel plaza across three infernal strata and vanquish Archon Malakor.
Micro Objectives: Eliminate each Stratum's hostile quota, harvest Ichor/Aegis caches, maintain a 5.0× combo chain, charge Ascension Overdrive, and enter the unsealed Colonnade Rift.

B. FLOW

Designed around a Sawtooth Pacing Curve: Plaza Insertion & Cover Assessment → Street Skirmishes → Multi-Directional Colonnade Siege → Overdrive Counter-Attack → Portal Unsealing & Tactical Breather.

C. DURATION

Single Stratum: 2–4 minutes.
Full 3-Stratum Campaign: 8–12 minutes.
Purgatory Blitz: Fast-paced 2.5–4 minute speedrun.
Endless Horde: 10–30+ minutes of high-score survival.

D. AVAILABILITY

Deployed globally on Vercel and hosted on GitHub as a WebGL Three.js application. Instant startup in any modern desktop browser with procedural PBR textures, particle smoke/sparks, and WebAudio synthesis.

E. RELATIONSHIP

All mechanics form a symbiotic closed loop: ADS Precision & Movement generate kills → Kills feed the Ascension Gauge & drop Ammo → Ascension Overdrive slows enemies & restores Aegis Armor → Survival unlocks the Citadel Portal.

F. PROGRESSION

Environmental & Threat Escalation:
• Stratum I (Citadel of Ash): Sulfurous ash-gold eclipse sky; Ash-Wretches & Cinder-Imps.
• Stratum II (Molten Basilica): Crimson blood-storm sky; introduces Iron-Gorgers & Void-Cantors.
• Stratum III (The Empyrean Breach): Violet void-hell sky; culminates in the Archon Malakor boss encounter.
