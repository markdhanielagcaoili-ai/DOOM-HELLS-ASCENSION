DOOM: HELL'S ASCENSION — 6-CRITERIA DESIGN DOCUMENT

Comprehensive architectural and mechanical specification fulfilling all six game development rubric criteria with original vertical-ascension mechanics, thermodynamic weapon symbiosis, and multi-layered progression.

ITEM 01

1. Gameplay

10 POINTS

Original Concept — Inverted Thermodynamic Ascension: Rather than descending into an underworld, the player awakens at the lowest frozen-ash floor of the Pit (The Nadir Vault) as a Resonance Warden. Because abyssal gravity crushes ordinary vessels, the only path to survival is upward kinetic propulsion powered by harvesting thermal soul-energy from fallen fiends.

  • Kinetic Resonance Harvesting: Eliminating enemies charges the Ascension Gauge (0–100%) and drops localized Vitality Ichor, Aegis Shards, and Ordnance Cells, rewarding aggressive forward momentum over passive cover-camping.
  • Chrono-Thermal Overdrive ([Q] / [E]): At 100% Ascension Resonance, the Warden triggers an 8-second temporal distortion that slows enemy movement and projectiles by 45%, doubles weapon fire rate, restores 25 Aegis Armor, and grants 2.0× outgoing damage.
  • Abyssal Phase-Dash ([SHIFT]): Omnidirectional high-impulse dash with 0.25s of invulnerability frames (i-frames) to weave through 3D projectile volleys and flank heavy brutes.
  • Four-Weapon Symbiotic Arsenal: Seamless switching between the Cinder-Lance (regenerative thermal sidearm), Hellfire Scatter-Maw (7-pellet pneumatic slag shotgun), Soul-Tether Pulse Rifle (high-RPM resonance builder), and the B.F.G. — Brimstone Fissure Generator (singularity shockwave cannon).
ITEM 02

2. Rules

10 POINTS

The simulation enforces deterministic combat, resource, and spatial containment rules that govern every player decision:

  • Rule 1 — Aegis Shield Partitioning: The player possesses 100 Vitality (HP) and up to 100 Aegis Armor. While Aegis Armor is active, it intercepts 65% of incoming damage, protecting core Vitality from lethal burst damage.
  • Rule 2 — Ordnance & Capacitor Conservation: Slot 1 (Cinder-Lance) has infinite self-charging thermal ammo to prevent soft-locks. Slot 2 consumes Slag Shells (1 per blast, max 60). Slot 3 consumes Plasma Cells (1 per bolt, max 200). Slot 4 (B.F.G.) consumes 25 Plasma Cells per cataclysmic detonation.
  • Rule 3 — Kill-Chain Decay Law: Consecutive kills within a 4.5-second window increment the Resonance & Score Multiplier by +0.5× (up to 5.0×). If 4.5 seconds pass without a kill, the multiplier resets to 1.0×.
  • Rule 4 — Stratum Containment Seal: Each Stratum's Ascension Rift Beacon remains locked in a dormant state until 100% of the Stratum's Hostile Quota is eliminated.
  • Rule 5 — Phase-Dash Cooldown Equilibrium: Each Phase-Dash requires a 1.4-second capacitor recharge before another dash can be executed.
ITEM 03

3. Victory and Loss Conditions

10 POINTS

🏆 VICTORY CONDITIONS

  • Stratum Clear: Purge 100% of hostile entities in the current Stratum to unseal the Ascension Beacon, then physically step into the golden-crimson rift.
  • Campaign Triumph (Total Ascension): Ascend through all three strata (Nadir Vault, Crucible Foundry, Empyrean Breach) and slay the apex boss, Archon Malakor, Sovereign of Cinders.
  • Purgatory Blitz Victory: Complete all three strata and slay the Archon before the Doomsday Clock reaches 00:00.0.
  • S+ Sovereign Mastery: Finish with a score exceeding 15,000 points and a peak multiplier of 5.0×.

💀 LOSS CONDITIONS

  • Vessel Shatter (Vitality ≤ 0): If melee strikes, fireball orbs, or void barrages reduce the Warden's Vitality to 0, the run terminates immediately in an ASCENSION FAILED state.
  • Chrono-Collapse (Time-Trial Mode): In Purgatory Blitz mode, allowing the countdown timer to expire (00:00.0) seals the tectonic plates permanently, resulting in instant defeat.
ITEM 04

4. Interactivity Modes

10 POINTS

DOOM: HELL'S ASCENSION implements both multi-modal User-System Interaction Layers and three distinct Playable Game Modes:

  • 1. First-Person Kinetic Combat Mode (Primary Perspective): Real-time 360° pointer-lock mouse aiming, WASD strafe-jumping/dashing, depth-shaded 3D raycast rendering, animated weapon viewmodels, and directional stereo/synth audio cues.
  • 2. Spatial Telemetry & Radar Mode (Simultaneous HUD): Real-time 2D top-down vector radar tracking corridor geometry, hostile threat vectors, supply caches, and the Ascension Beacon alongside peripheral damage/overdrive shaders.
  • 3. Playable Mode A — Campaign Ascension: Structured 3-Stratum narrative progression culminating in the multi-phase boss battle against Archon Malakor.
  • 4. Playable Mode B — Endless Abyssal Horde: Infinite wave-survival mode with dynamically scaling enemy count, speed, and periodic arena supply drops.
  • 5. Playable Mode C — Purgatory Blitz (Speedrun): High-velocity countdown mode where every kill injects +2.5 seconds directly into the survival timer.
ITEM 05

5. Challenges

10 POINTS

Players are tested across five interconnected skill dimensions:

  • 1. Multi-Archetype Target Prioritization: Simultaneous waves combine fast melee rushers (Ash-Wretches), strafing fireball lobbers (Cinder-Imps), heavily armored tanks (Iron-Gorgers), and teleporting void casters (Void-Cantors).
  • 2. 3D Projectile Evasion & Cover Usage: Enemy fireballs and void bolts travel through 3D space; players must read projectile trajectories, time their [SHIFT] Phase-Dashes, and use obsidian pillars as physical cover.
  • 3. Ammunition Triage: Balancing high-consumption Plasma Cells between sustained Pulse Rifle fire and reserving 25 cells for emergency B.F.G. crowd-clears.
  • 4. Momentum vs. Survival Risk: Keeping the 5.0× Kill-Chain Multiplier alive requires relentless aggression within 4.5-second windows, tempting players into dangerous crossfire.
  • 5. Apex Boss Mechanics (Archon Malakor): In Stratum III, the Archon unleashes tri-fan plasma volleys and summons reinforcement waves at 50% HP, testing mastery of Overdrive timing and arena kiting.
ITEM 06

6. Structure (Objectives, Flow, Duration, Availability, Relationship, Progression)

10 POINTS

A. OBJECTIVES

Macro Objective: Escape the Nadir Vault by ascending through three infernal strata and defeating Archon Malakor.
Micro Objectives: Eliminate each Stratum's hostile quota, harvest Ichor/Aegis caches, maintain a 5.0× combo chain, charge Ascension Overdrive, and enter the unsealed Rift Beacon.

B. FLOW

Designed around a Sawtooth Pacing Curve: Safe Drop-In & Orientation → Corridor Skirmishes → Multi-Directional Arena Crescendo → Overdrive Counter-Attack → Beacon Unsealing & Tactical Breather before ascending to the next Stratum.

C. DURATION

Single Stratum: 2–4 minutes.
Full 3-Stratum Campaign: 8–12 minutes.
Purgatory Blitz: Fast-paced 2.5–4 minute speedrun.
Endless Horde: 10–30+ minutes of high-score survival.

D. AVAILABILITY

Deployed globally on Vercel and hosted on GitHub as a zero-dependency HTML5/JavaScript application. Instant startup in any modern desktop browser with procedural textures and WebAudio synthesis (no external downloads required).

E. RELATIONSHIP

All mechanics form a symbiotic closed loop: Aggression generates kills → Kills feed the Ascension Gauge & drop Ammo → Ascension Overdrive slows enemies & restores Aegis Armor → Survival unlocks the Stratum Beacon.

F. PROGRESSION

Environmental & Threat Escalation:
• Stratum I (The Nadir Vault): Frozen ash walls; Ash-Wretches & Cinder-Imps.
• Stratum II (The Crucible Foundry): Molten brass walls; introduces Iron-Gorgers & Void-Cantors.
• Stratum III (The Empyrean Breach): Blood-sanctum architecture; culminates in the Archon Malakor boss encounter.
